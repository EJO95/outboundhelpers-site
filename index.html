How it works

  1. 1

    Domain & mailbox setup

    We configure sending domains and mailboxes with correct SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records from day one, so your mail is authenticated before a single message goes out.

  2. 2

    List building & verification

    Prospect lists are built against the criteria you give us and run through verification to remove invalid, risky, or undeliverable addresses before anything is sent.

  3. 3

    Sequenced sending with warmup

    New domains and mailboxes are warmed up gradually. Sending volume and pacing ramp up over time, following patterns that mailbox providers recognize as normal, human-run outreach.

  4. 4

    Reply handling & reporting

    Replies, bounces, and opt-outs are tracked and routed. You get a clear, regular report on what was sent, what landed, and who replied.

Principles

  • No scraped or stolen lists — lists are built and verified against criteria you set.
  • No spam tactics. Volume and pacing are set to protect deliverability and sender reputation, not to maximize blast size.
  • Opt-outs are honored promptly and permanently.
  • Sending practices are CAN-SPAM and GDPR-aware, including accurate sender identification and a working way to opt out.
